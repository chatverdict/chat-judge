# Chat Judge

Judge your group chat - free, 100% local in your browser.

Drop a WhatsApp, iMessage or Telegram export and get a funny verdict for every person in the chat. The analysis runs entirely in your browser: your messages are never uploaded anywhere.

**Try it:** https://chatverdict.surge.sh/try.html

## How it works

- All parsing and statistics run client-side in JavaScript (see `local-engine.js`)
- Real names are replaced with aliases on your device before any analysis
- Verdicts are derived from measured statistics only (message share, word counts, laughter, reply speed, question rate and more)
- No accounts, no servers, no tracking of chat content
